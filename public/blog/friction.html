Friction

2026-11-27

The AI revolution keeps unfolding and reaching new highs in mathematics, biology, engineering, design, well... on everything. People in contrast seem to be reaching new lows, the reason for it might be associated with how we built a society around the instrumentalisation of humans or the attention economy or its ugly predecessor surveillance capitalism. I may one day rant more about that, but not today.

Today, I want to navigate my scrambled thoughts on Cognitive Degradation the uglier sibling of Addy Osmani's Cognitive Surrender. In his blog he describes surrendering as the act of leaving the decision making to the AI.

This starts happening mostly in two scenarios:

  • Ignorance, the user doesn't know the problem space so they have to take the AI's response at face value
  • Laziness, thinking consumes energy -- brainey doesn't likey

It's even worse when the two combine. The effects of this are not visible immediately, you push the AI output and that becomes someone else's problem and for all it's worth the output looks good. Your brain learned a new thing "I can avoid the work and get the cookie", which can be getting the task done and going back to scrolling X or TikTok so we can finish the frontal lobe with some rosemary at the frying pan.

This continues, days, weeks, months. Eventually your AI provider is down and the task still needs to be done but now things changed, it's not that you can do the task at a slow pace, you can't do it anymore.

Your brain rejects the ask entirely and makes you feel anxious. Why? Easy, that will cost a lot of energy and it learned a new pathway that doesn't involve friction. The one thing you were capable of doing you no longer remember how to, you have to pay your provider to do it for you.

But the brain is not like a circuit and you can recover, it will take time, your brain will fight you every step of the way but you can learn and think again.

And before I leave for today, I know what you might be thinking. "With AI I can do a thousand things previously I couldn't" But you can't really, you are suffering the delusion of the Dunning-Kruger effect. You became the stochastic parrot, without it you can't recreate the output even with time.

I love using AI, I use it every day, but I hold myself accountable every day.
